NP Membership Benefits

  • Free listing in the online business directory
  • Invitations to Chamber networking events
  • Access to volunteer and community partnership opportunities
  • Newsletter mentions for organization announcements

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Everything in NP Membership
  • Standard directory listing with business description
  • Discounted admission to Chamber training workshops
  • One free ribbon-cutting event coordination per year

Silver Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Bronze Membership
  • Featured "Silver Spotlight" rotation on the home page
  • Two complimentary tickets to the annual Chamber gala
  • Discounted booth pricing at community events
  • Quarterly promotional email inclusion

Gold Membership Benefits

  • Everything in Silver Membership
  • Premium "Gold Spotlight" placement on the home page
  • Free booth at all Chamber-hosted events
  • Logo placement on the Chamber website and printed materials
  • Priority scheduling for training and advertising slots
  • Unlimited ribbon-cutting event coordination
